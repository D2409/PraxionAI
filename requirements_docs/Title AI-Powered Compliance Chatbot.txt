Title: AI-Powered Compliance Chatbot
Description:  Develop a chatbot that can answer E&C-related questions, provide guidance on ethical dilemmas, and offer real-time advice on compliance issues. The Chatbot would be tailored to a specific company and be tied to the specific policies, procedures as well as ethics and compliance program of the company. The tool must be customizable by us in order to feed it the proper documents and information on which the chatbot would be working from. The application would then have a production version for us to input the right datasets and conditions and then a user version that could be deployed to organizations to use with their employees and stakeholders and accessible via desktop or mobile devices. It would also be tied into the whistleblowing channels of the company. Using Natural language, end users could have access to answers that are based and linked to the organizations policy and procedures and international best practices and standards.
Key Features:  
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) for understanding user queries.
- A database of regulations, case studies, and best practices.
- Integration with company-specific E&C policies and procedures.
- Machine learning to improve responses over time based on user interactions.
